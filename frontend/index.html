Our Ethical Mission

Revolutionizing Menstrual Health,

BYHARIANS was born from a simple yet radical belief: women deserve intimate care that is luxuriously soft, medically safe, and completely harmonious with the earth.

12 Billion Plastic Pads Are Discarded Every Year.

Traditional sanitary napkins are made of up to 90% petroleum plastic. They sit in landfills and ocean waterways for over 500 years, fragmenting into toxic microplastics that enter the food chain.

We engineered a revolutionary alternative using organic bamboo fibers and plant starch biopolymers that return to the soil in just 180 days, leaving zero microplastic footprint.

Our 180-Day Soil Journey

Day 1: Ethical Bamboo Harvesting

Fast-growing organic bamboo shoots harvested without pesticides or defoliants.

Day 30: Zero-Bleach Closed-Loop Processing

Bamboo transformed into soft fibers using 100% steam without chlorine or harsh chemical baths.

Day 180: Full Natural Decomposition

Placed in soil or compost, the pad breaks down completely into rich nutrient compost.
