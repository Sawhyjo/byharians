EKAPADS 5-Layer Natural Organic Structure

Every EKAPADS sanitary pad is engineered with 5 natural plant biomass layers that are eco-friendly, microplastic-free, and provide maximum comfort and leak protection:

  • 1. Top Sheet (Biodegradable Bamboo Liner): Top layer crafted from cool, silky, hypoallergenic organic bamboo fibers, keeping intimate skin dry and irritation-free.
  • 2. ADL / Acquisition Distribution Layer (Banana Cellulose + Kenaf Fiber): Fluid distribution layer blending banana stem cellulose and kenaf fibers, evenly dispersing liquid across the core without bunching.
  • 3. Absorbent Core (Banana Cellulose + CMC): High-absorbency core using natural banana cellulose and plant-based Carboxymethyl Cellulose (CMC) that is safe and synthetic-polymer free.
  • 4. Secondary Layer (Biodegradable Bamboo Liner): Supporting secondary layer to lock in moisture and provide extra leak-proof protection during active movements.
  • 5. Backsheet (Seaweed Bioplastic Film): Impermeable backsheet crafted from breathable seaweed bioplastic film that is 100% bio-degradable in soil.

ABOUT US — OUR MISSION & PROBLEM STATEMENT

EKAPADS: Circular Menstrual Care

Solving the crisis of non-biodegradable plastic menstrual waste in Indonesia through bio-engineered agricultural waste pads and 100% plant-based materials.

Our Vision

A zero-waste community sanitation ecosystem where menstrual hygiene nurtures female dignity without polluting Indonesia's waterways and soil systems.

Our Mission

Transforming agricultural bio-waste (banana pseudostem & kenaf fibers) into high-performance 100% biodegradable pads to eliminate single-use plastic waste.

Core Values

From Nature, Back to Nature: Responsible consumption, circular product design, vulvar skin safety (0% PU/PVC), and behavior change education.

THE PROBLEM IN INDONESIA

Plastic Menstrual Waste & Unmanaged Water Pollution.

Conventional single-use pads consist of up to 90% synthetic plastic, taking up to 500 years to decompose in landfills (UNEP, 2021).

In low-income peri-urban communities lacking municipal waste infrastructure, where national monitoring shows over 30% of waste remains unmanaged (KLHK/SIPSN, 2023), disposed pads are routinely dumped in rivers or open burn piles, choking drainage channels, elevating microplastic pollution and flood risks.

OUR PROPOSED SOLUTION

EKAPADS Circular Sanitation Ecosystem

  • CORE Banana Pseudostem Cellulose: High-absorbency core engineered from abundant agricultural waste.
  • ADL Kenaf Fiber Layer: Rapid fluid distribution and superior leak protection without synthetic liners.
  • DISPOSAL Biodegradable Starter Pouch: Enables hygienic, plastic-free disposal that breaks down naturally in soil.
  • DECOMPOSITION 100% Soil Biodegradation: Zero microplastics, returning natural plant nutrients safely back to the earth.

INNOVATION & DIFFERENTIATION

Beyond Material Substitution: Circular Sanitation

Repurposing Indonesia's Agricultural Waste

Existing biodegradable pads mainly focus on material substitution while disposal practices remain unchanged. EKAPADS goes beyond by engineering banana pseudostem cellulose into an ultra-absorbent core and kenaf fiber into an Acquisition Distribution Layer (ADL) for rapid fluid distribution and leak protection.

Two-Fold Disposal Innovation:

1. Biodegradable Disposal Pouch: Functions as a decomposition starter, eliminating extra plastic wrapping.
2. Zero Microplastic Soil Return: Used pads break down naturally without polluting local landfills or rivers.
